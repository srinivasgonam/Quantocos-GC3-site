    What GC³ is

    Most capability centers do the grunt work. Ours does the thinking.

    A traditional GCC takes transactional work — tickets, back office, maintenance — and leaves the strategy, the brand and the customer experience with somebody else. GC³ takes the whole function: the thinking, the making, and the systems that keep both consistent.

    Practically, that means a client does not commission a campaign and then commission a website and then commission a strategy deck from three suppliers who never speak. They subscribe to a pod, and the strategy, the creative and the systems come out of the same room, on the same calendar, measured in the same numbers.

    Not this

    A staff-augmentation shop

    We do not sell you a designer's timesheet and leave the outcomes to you. A pod owns the outcome and the calendar.

    This

    An embedded capability

    A team that plugs into your tools, learns your brand once, and produces every month without re-briefing. It is embedding, not outsourcing.

    The pod model

    A pod is a micro-enterprise, not a department

    Each pod carries its own manager, its own specialists and its own agents, and is measured on the work it ships rather than the hours it logs. Pods serve a small client group; dedicated pods are available when the volume justifies it.

    Creative Pod — 3 people + 2 agents Digital Pod — 5 people + 4 agents Strategy Pod — 7 people + 6 agents
    Why the model works
    • Built for speed — no bureaucratic handoffs between four vendors.
    • Quality comes from shared design systems, guild standards and process frameworks, not from heroics.
    • Cost is predictable — a subscription and a credit pool, not an estimate that becomes an argument.
    • Every sprint trains the automation layer, so month six costs less to run than month one.

    How a pod runs

    Five steps, then it repeats every month

    1. 01

      Discovery & alignment

      We learn your brand, tone, tools and workflows. You meet the manager who will run your pod.

      Client + pod manager
    2. 02

      Pod integration

      The pod joins your systems — Slack, ClickUp, Miro, your drive — and your brand assets are taken into your own knowledge base.

      Pod manager + technology specialist
    3. 03

      Sprint planning

      Deliverables are broken into weekly cycles with a credit estimate per item, so you can see the cost before it is spent.

      Whole pod
    4. 04

      Execution & review

      Agents draft, specialists finish, you approve. Revision rounds sit inside the sprint rather than becoming a change order.

      Client + pod
    5. 05

      Reporting & optimization

      Every month closes with the credit ledger, what shipped, what it moved, and what we would change next month.

      Pod manager + AIops
    Human-in-the-loop

    Half the work is done by agents. Half by a named specialist.

    A pod is not a team of people using tools, and it is not an agent running unattended. Agents take the brief, draft the documentation and produce the first pass; named specialists review, correct and finish it. Nothing ships that a human has not signed off.

    How the work is divided

    One main agent, subagents, microagents and nanoagents

    One agent holds the brief and the brand, then distributes the work. The messages between agents are carried and recorded, so a specialist can audit any branch of the tree rather than trusting a summary.

    GC³ Portal

    One dashboard for both sides of the engagement

    The portal is where onboarding, requests, credits, billing and team access live. Client side and pod side see the same request, at the same time.

    Book a portal walkthrough
    Client admin

    Onboarding and requests

    Submit the brief, brand assets and business context once. After that, anyone in your business can raise a request — a deck, a campaign, a landing page, a video — from the same dashboard.

    Client admin

    Users, billing and credits

    Add or remove your own users, manage the subscription and add-ons, buy credits for special requests, and see the ledger in real time.

    Pod side

    Delegation

    The pod manager sees every request, estimates the credits, and assigns it to the right specialist or agent in the pod.

    Internal oversight

    Pod health

    Our super-admin layer: workload and health across every pod, assignment of clients to pods, regional oversight and performance analytics for area leaders.

    What we are not

    Plainly, so nobody is surprised later

    Not a BPO

    We do not sell seats on a process you have already designed. We design the work and then run it.

    Not a dev shop

    Engineering is one surface of one product. It is not the business, and it is not sold as a body shop.

    Not a call center

    There is no volume metric on this engagement. Output is judged on whether it moved something.

    Not unlimited

    Credits are finite and visible. A pod that runs out of credits tells you, rather than silently absorbing the overrun.

    Not fully automated

    Agents draft and repeat. They do not decide. Every deliverable has a human name attached to it.

    Not yet complete

    Several products in our stack are in active development. We label them as such on the product pages rather than implying they are finished.
