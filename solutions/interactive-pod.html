    Interactive Pod

    The month, drawn

    Interactive Pod, as a month of credits

    In short

    The Interactive Pod is a custom-scoped Quantocos team that builds immersive and interactive work: real-estate visualisation and 3D walkthroughs, browser-based product configurators, scrollytelling microsites, VR showrooms, XR training modules and game-engine prototypes, scoped and quoted on request.

    Key facts
    Price$8,000/month, at the same tier as the Automation Pod
    Credit rateSet out in your engagement letter
    Delivers3D and XR, configurators, interactive microsites
    PipelineSpline, Figma, Framer, Webflow, Verge3D, 8th Wall, Godot, GDevelop, Blender
    Typical useReal-estate launches, product explanation, training
    OutputWeb-first, with device and performance budgets agreed up front

    What the money buys each month

    • Real-estate visualisation and renders
    • Interactive 3D web experiences

    • Interactive microsites and scrollytelling
    • VR showrooms and virtual walkthroughs
    • XR training modules
    • Game-engine prototypes and proof of concept
    • Motion and 3D asset production
    What it replaces

    The hire or the supplier this stands in for

    A specialist 3D or XR studio, and the coordination cost of briefing one separately from the brand and campaign work.

    A product, a property or a process that cannot be understood from a photograph gets explained in prose, badly and at length. Interactive work fixes that, but most buyers cannot buy it: the studios that do it are priced for enterprise brands, and the agencies that understand the brand cannot build the thing.

    How the month runs

    Four weeks, then it repeats

    The full pod workflow
    01Sprint 1Asset inventory and feasibility — what exists, what has to be modelled
    02Sprint 2Core build: geometry, lighting and the interaction model
    03Sprint 3Performance and device coverage, so it runs on a mid-range phone
    04Sprint 4Handover with source files and a device performance budget
    The credit maths

    How a scoped engagement is estimated

    Estimated from the asset inventory. Model production and optimization typically runs seventy to ninety credits, the interaction build sixty to eighty, and performance work twenty to thirty. The estimate is fixed before the first sprint rather than tracked during it.

    Working with clients abroad

    Built to be bought from the US, the UK, the EU and ANZ

    Everything a buyer outside India needs to clear before the first conversation, stated up front rather than in a proposal.

    Your hours, not ours

    Pods are staffed to overlap your business day, and every pod has a named manager who answers inside it.

    Agreed before the first brief

    An NDA and a data-processing agreement are signed before work starts, and you are told where your files are stored and which people can reach them.

    You own the work

    Copyright and intellectual property in everything we produce assign to you.

    Month to month

    The subscription pods run without a lock-in beyond the notice period. No setup fee, no exit fee, and the minimum term is stated before you sign.

    One number, in dollars

    Invoiced in USD, one monthly figure, one point of contact. No per-seat uplift.
