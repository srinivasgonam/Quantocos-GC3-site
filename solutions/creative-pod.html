    The month, drawn

    Creative Pod, as a month of credits

    In short

    The Creative Pod is a monthly subscription creative team from Quantocos, priced at $3,000/month for 120 credits. It consists of one pod manager, two human specialists and two AI agents, and it produces social media content, brand assets, copywriting, short-form video and sales collateral for foreign SMB and mid-market businesses. Credits are the unit the portal bills in.

    What the money buys each month

    • Social media content and scheduling

    • Short-form video and motion
    • Presentation and sales decks
    • Photography direction and retouching
    • Campaign key visuals

    What it replaces

    The hire or the supplier this stands in for

    The in-house social and creative content function — this replaces a function, not a set of individual deliverables.

    Most small businesses need the same thing every month — a steady output of content — and most cannot justify hiring a designer, a copywriter and an editor to get it. Agencies solve it at a price and a pace that only make sense for campaigns, and freelancers leave nothing behind when they go.

    How the month runs

    Four weeks, then it repeats

    01Week 1The calendar is agreed and briefs go in through the portal

    04Week 4The month is reported: credits spent, what shipped, next month's calendar
    The credit maths

    How 120 credits actually get spent

    A worked example of a creative month: twelve social posts at two credits each, four short videos at eight, one campaign key visual at ten, six email or web assets at three, and two deck refreshes at six — ninety-six of the hundred and twenty credits, leaving twenty-four for whatever the month throws up.

    Buying questions

    What to settle before you subscribe

    The Creative Pod costs $3,000/month and includes 120 credits. One credit equals the unit the portal bills in. Onboarding, brief clarification, asset handover and the monthly pod review are never drawn from those credits.

    One pod manager, two human specialists, two AI agents, 120 credits of work per month, and every software license the pod needs — design and creative applications, AI generation, stock imagery and fonts. There are no add-on fees.

    AI agents draft and repeat the repeatable work; human specialists review, correct and finish it. Nothing is delivered that a named human has not signed off, and the pod manager is accountable for the outcome.

    A pod is a function with a calendar and a manager, not a series of tickets. It is priced as a subscription with a visible credit pool, so the second month is cheaper to run than the first and scope is settled before work begins.

    Paid media management, SEO and strategy. Those sit in the Growth and Strategy catalogs, which the Digital Pod and Strategy Pod include. The Creative Pod is the production layer only.

    Yes. Agencies and consultancies run pods white-label for their own clients. The engagement is with Quantocos, the client relationship stays with you.

    Working with clients abroad

    Built to be bought from the US, the UK, the EU and ANZ

    Everything a buyer outside India needs to clear before the first conversation, stated up front rather than in a proposal.

    Your hours, not ours

    Pods are staffed to overlap your business day, and every pod has a named manager who answers inside it.

    Agreed before the first brief

    An NDA and a data-processing agreement are signed before work starts, and you are told where your files are stored and which people can reach them.

    You own the work

    Copyright and intellectual property in everything we produce assign to you.

    Month to month

    The subscription pods run without a lock-in beyond the notice period. No setup fee, no exit fee, and the minimum term is stated before you sign.

    One number, in dollars

    Invoiced in USD, one monthly figure, one point of contact. No per-seat uplift.
