    Life at Quantocos

    Small teams, visible work, no hiding in a department

    A pod has a manager, a handful of specialists and a set of agents. What you make is attributable to you, reviewed by your guild, and seen by a client — usually within the same week.

    Real ownership early

    New joiners ship client-facing work in their first quarter, with a named mentor and a review that looks at judgment rather than hours.

    Guild development

    A guild leader is accountable for your craft. Critique sessions, skill paths and competition between pods are part of the working week, not an annual event.

    Learn and be assessed

    Guild-led development, assessed rather than assumed, with credits for skills you can prove.

    Credits you can monetise

    Time saved is what earns credit here, not hours logged. Those credits convert into rewards, and the leaderboard is visible across the group.

    Hiring process

    01 — Application, reviewed by a guild leader rather than a recruiter

    02 — A craft conversation, with work you are proud of

    03 — A paid working session on a real problem (half a day)

    04 — Meet the pod manager you would work with

    05 — Offer within five working days of the final session

    We give written feedback to everyone who reaches stage 03, whether or not we hire them.
