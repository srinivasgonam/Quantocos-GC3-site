    Contract pack

    The documents, before the invoice.

    What we sign, what each document covers, when you receive it, and what the site actually runs on. Procurement should not have to ask twice.

    In short

    The pack contains seven documents: a mutual NDA, an engagement letter, a master services agreement, a data processing agreement, an IP assignment, an AI assurance note and a completed security questionnaire. The NDA, security questionnaire and AI assurance note are available before you are a customer. There is no termination fee and no annual lock-in.

    The contract pack — what is in it
    DocumentWhat it coversWhen you receive it
    Mutual NDAConfidentiality both ways. Covers what is shared before an engagement exists: briefs, roadmaps, brand material and commercial terms.On request, before the first call if you want it
    Engagement letterThe terms for your engagement specifically: which pod, the monthly fee, the credit pool, the overage rate and the notice period.At the point of order
    Master services agreementThe standing terms: scope and change control, liability, indemnity, confidentiality, term and termination, and governing law.With the engagement letter

    Commercial terms

    The terms in plain words

    The summary below is what the engagement letter states. If a summary and the signed document ever disagree, the signed document wins and we will correct the summary.

    Commercial terms, summarised
    TermMonth to month. There is no annual lock-in on a pod.
    NoticeOne month, at the month boundary. Stated in the engagement letter.
    CreditsThe monthly pool does not roll over. What is unused at month end is not carried forward.
    OverageWork beyond the pool is quoted at the published overage rate before it is started, never billed silently.
    Price changesA pod price changes for you only at a month boundary, and only after written notice.
    IPOutput transfers on payment. Background methods and platforms remain ours.
    DataProcessed under the DPA, and not used to train shared or foundation models.

    What is not in the pack

    What we cannot put in front of you yet

    A procurement review is also a test of what a supplier admits. These absences are stated rather than left to be discovered.

    • No ISO 27001 certificate
    • No SOC 2 report
    • No client references, because there are no clients yet
    • No service-credit schedule for missed turnaround, because we do not publish turnaround commitments we cannot yet evidence
    • No audited financial statements in the pack; the entity and its registration are stated in the legal pages
    Questions

    Contract questions, answered

    Can we sign our own paper instead of yours?

    Ask. Where your procurement requires your master agreement, we will review it and say plainly what we can and cannot accept. A small company should not promise to absorb liability it cannot insure, and we would rather decline a clause at the start than dispute it later.

    Do you hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2?

    Not yet. The company was incorporated in 2026 and holds neither certificate. The control set is documented, the security page states each control and its status, and we will publish the certificates when they exist rather than imply them today.

    Is there a termination fee?

    No. You stop at the month boundary with one month of notice. There is no exit fee, no unused-credit clawback and no minimum term beyond the month you are in.

    Who signs for Quantocos?

    The engagement is with Quantocos Global Systems LLP, the parent entity. The name of the signatory and their authority are stated in the engagement letter.

    Can we review the pack before we are a customer?

    Yes. The DPA, the security questionnaire and the AI assurance note are available before any engagement exists. Ask through the contact form and say which documents your reviewer needs.

    Next step

    Send your reviewer the pack before the commercial conversation.

    The documents are ready. If a control or a clause is missing, the answer will be specific.

    Before you sign

    The five questions buyers ask.

    What happens if the work takes longer than the estimate?

    The estimate is issued before work starts and variance is capped at 15% unless you approve more. Anything beyond that needs a new estimate you accept.

    Do unused credits roll over?

    No. Credits reset monthly and the balance is not carried into the next month.

    What happens when scope changes mid-request?

    Scope creep is a new request with its own estimate. It is never silently absorbed into the current one or billed without agreement.

    What do priority and rush cost?

    Priority is 1.5x credits with turnaround at half; rush is 2.0x with turnaround at a quarter, capped at 20% of the monthly pool and dependent on capacity.

    What is not charged to credits?

    Onboarding, brief clarification, asset handover and the monthly pod review. Paid media spend is paid by you directly to the platforms.
