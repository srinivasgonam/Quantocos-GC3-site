    Saltpetre, as you would meet it

    Feed it everything and it separates the signal from the noise.

    In short

    Saltpetre is the research and data surface of Prism, in development. It ingests URLs, articles, images and video, filters noise from signal, and turns that signal into datasets, MCPs, plugins and skills — which can then be used to fine-tune or train custom models.

    The workflow it replaces

    Four tools and a file-transfer ritual

    A folder of articles, recordings and pages, plus months of manual labelling before any of it is usable.

    What this surface does

    Every capability inside it

    • Ingest URLs, articles, images and video
    • Noise extraction and signal filtering
    • Build MCPs, plugins, skills and datasets from the signal
    • Composite data fragments into new modules
    • Fine-tune and train custom models on the datasets it produced

    Useful training data is not lying around. It exists as a pile of articles, recordings and pages that nobody has time to read, and the gap between that pile and a usable dataset is months of labelling.

    Saltpetre is the research and data surface in Prism, in development. It ingests URLs, articles, images and video, filters the noise out of them, and turns what is left into datasets, MCPs, plugins and skills.

    Fine-tuning and training custom models, building agent skills, and feeding the group's own tools — including Caksh, which applies the same compositing idea to research data.

    It ingests content a client is entitled to use, including their own archives and licensed sources. Data provenance is part of the surface, not an afterthought.
